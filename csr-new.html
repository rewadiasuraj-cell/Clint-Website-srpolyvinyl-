Every child deserves a childhood


Future generations can go for more when every little step takes them closer to their dreams. We strongly believe that no deserving student should be get anything less than a quality education for the lack of money. To put this belief into action we pay the school fees for the underprivileged students of Shri. Parshwanath Jain Vidyalaya, coming from rural areas who belong to various castes.

Touching lives in more ways than one


Everyone deserves hope – a second life in this life. We endeavor to contribute too. We empower Nav Adarsh Foundation to go for more in their noble work, by providing feed for Gaushalas, and food kits & medical support to underprivileged people. We believe that it’s our duty to support the good intentions that strengthens the nation and makes world a better place.

No compromise on hygiene, and environment. Period.


Every adolescent girl child has a right to menstrual hygiene. To ensure this right is exercised, Sushila Parmar Group, in association with the Rotary Club of Poona West, distributed reusable sanitary napkins in schools in rural and slum areas and also spread awareness about periods and menstrual health.

Thinking about those thinking about others.


Donating blood is the noblest act, and blood banks are carrying out a great responsibility of collecting blood and making it available to those in need. In association with the Rotary Club of Poona West, Sushila Parmar Group donated Donor chairs to Jankalyan Blood Bank. A small contribution to the blood collection in Pune.

Uniting India through Education


On understanding the spirit of PSVP's 25 inspiring years of bringing India together through education, we strongly felt like joining hands with them. We learnt about the sufferings the underdeveloped locals in North East region face, and aim to improve the quality of life and education for them. We have provided benches, tables for classrooms, and a washing machine for their hostel, trying to make life slightly easier for this region.

Empowering children, no matter where they come from


We at Sushila Parmar Group identify underprivileged children in slum areas in various parts of Pune and conduct free classes for them in the evening. They are also imparted with soft skills training and lessons in Civics to create awareness and disciplined citizens of tomorrow.

We don't just say "Get well soon", we help them to


In a world full of challenges, we wish to support those who struggle to support themselves. And we do so by providing free medicines to the underprivileged people admitted to Vivekanand Hospital District Sangli, Maharashtra, managed by Vivekanand Vaidyak Pratishthan. When people are suffering is the time when they need support. We do our bit to reduce their pain and help in their faster recovery.

Doing something special for special kids


Believing the biggest disability is a negative mindset - we do our best to support the training & studies for visually impaired and specially abled students in various trades. We are grateful to this Apang Rojgar Udyog Va Tantrik Prashikshan Kendra (Handicap Center) in Pune for doing such great work. To make their journey easier, we donated a school bus tailored for these special kids. They deserve to travel safely and in absolute comfort.

FEB 2018 - Making a Difference


It feels great to be a part of a world record that makes a difference to the world. Sushila Parmar International is immensely proud to have associated with PLASTINIDIA’s initiative of the
World’s Largest T-shirt from 100% Recycled Plastic
The magnanimous t-shirt helped to make t-shirts for over 10,000 underprivileged children.

Igniting the future for 100 students in Rajasthan


Education has the power to shape the future of individuals, families, and societies. Sushila Parmar Group has taken this cause to heart.
We have sponsored the complete education for 100 needy students at a high-standard CBSE school in Badamia by the name of Smt. Umbraobai Dhanrajji Badamia English School. There's no better joy than putting their lives on a better path.
