And that's just the beginning

Our small beginnings are our inspiration to Go for More and have led us to think bigger, always. Step by step, in 20 years, the Sushila Parmar Group has changed the polymer raw material procurement landscape in India to become India’s largest distribution network.

Be it introducing transparency or commitment to delivery, be it bringing mutual respect to the trade or bringing ease and convenience with technology, we’ve redefined the way business is done in this industry.

We are now taking this spirit to the global markets and are all set to make a mark on the world.

What started as a dream to do something different has reached an unimagined milestone. Our humble beginnings have now reached all the way to countries abroad, where clients trust Parmar to deliver on commitments univocally.

Challenging the traditional ways of business for over 20 years, Parmar has taken many important steps to transform the polymer raw material. supply chain dynamics to making sourcing an easy, convenient and a pleasant experience.

Along the way, as any business that puts their heart and soul into what they do, Parmar has transformed associations into relationships, team members into family members and business partners into growth partners.
