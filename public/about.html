Our

profile

Established in 2001, S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd. has emerged as a leading distributor and stockist of a wide range of chemical products for the PVC industry.

We are authorized distributors of PAPEX™, Chemplast Sanmar and Payal Polyplast, catering to the artificial leather, pipe, footwear and allied plastic industries. Our long-standing relationships with reputed global manufacturers ensure consistent quality, reliability and competitive sourcing.

We work closely with our group company SSF Polymers Ltd., a synthetic leather producer known for innovation and sustainable practices, running a state-of-the-art production line designed by Primec, Italy.

Our vision

To be India's most trusted PVC resin distributor and chemical stockist — a company that industries grow with, and partners rely on.

We see a future where S. R. Polyvinyl is an essential pillar of the Artificial Leather (Rexine), Pipe, Footwear and PVC manufacturing ecosystem — connecting the world's finest manufacturers to India's most ambitious producers.

Quality

assurance

Quality assurance is a systematic, planned set of activities that prevents defects and ensures every lot meets the specified standards and our customers' requirements.

1

Raw material inspection

Particle size, chemical composition, moisture and impurities checked on arrival.

2

Process control

Manufacturer process parameters verified against specifications.

3

In-process testing

Viscosity, particle size and bulk density monitored throughout.

4

Quality control testing

Thermal stability, colour consistency and strength of finished product.

5

Final inspection

Stage-by-stage evaluation to catch any deviation before dispatch.
