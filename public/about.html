ABOUT S. R. POLYVINYL

A strong foundation.
A shared future.

Established in 2001, S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd. has grown from a regional stockist into one of North India's premier authorized distributors and importers of PVC resins, plasticizers, and specialty polymer chemicals.

Headquartered in New Delhi, we provide a reliable raw material backbone to manufacturers across Artificial Leather Cloth (Rexine), PVC pipes and profiles, footwear, wire & cables, and allied plastics.
