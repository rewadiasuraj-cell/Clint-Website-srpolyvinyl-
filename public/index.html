Established in 2001, S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd. is a trusted distributor, importer and stockist of premium chemical products, proudly serving the Artificial Leather Cloth (Rexine) and plastics industry for over two decades.

As an authorized distributor of PAPEX™, Chemplast Sanmar and Payal Polyplast — some of India's most respected names in PVC — we bring together an unmatched portfolio of products for the artificial leather, pipe, footwear and allied plastic industries.

Our strong, long-standing relationships with reputed global manufacturers ensure consistent quality, reliability and competitive sourcing. Together with our group company SSF Polymers Ltd. — a manufacturer of artificial leather on a state-of-the-art line designed by Primec, Italy — we understand our customers' processes from the inside.

At S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd., we go beyond transactions — we build partnerships, and every customer we serve is a testament to that commitment.

Integrity in every deal. Quality without compromise.

Connecting the world's finest manufacturers to India's most ambitious producers.
