Established in 2001, S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd. is a trusted distributor, importer and stockist of premium chemical products, proudly serving the Artificial Leather Cloth (Rexine) and plastics industry for over two decades.

As an authorized distributor of PAPEX™, Chemplast Sanmar and Payal Polyplast — some of India's most respected names in PVC — we bring together an unmatched portfolio of products for the artificial leather, pipe, footwear and allied plastic industries.
