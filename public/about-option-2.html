01 / WHO WE ARE

Big possibilities start
with the right material.

Established in 2001 in New Delhi, S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd. has been at the forefront of chemical and polymer distribution in India. Over the last 25+ years, we have played a pivotal role in enabling India's artificial leather cloth (rexine), footwear, and plastics manufacturers to compete on global standards.

Rather than acting merely as middlemen, we serve as technical solution providers — analyzing formulation requirements, viscosity benchmarks, and plasticizer efficiency to help factories achieve lower rejection rates and higher production throughput.

04 / GROUP CAPABILITIES

Synergy, Trust & Accreditation

In addition to our distribution network, group company SSF Polymers Ltd. operates high-precision synthetic leather manufacturing lines designed by Primec, Italy. This practical production insight informs every recommendation our distribution team provides.
