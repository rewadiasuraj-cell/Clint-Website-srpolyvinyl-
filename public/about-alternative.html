Who we are

Focused on
your production.

Established in 2001, S. R. Polyvinyl Ltd. is a New Delhi-based distributor, importer and stockist of PVC resins, plasticizers and allied chemicals. We serve manufacturers of artificial leather, pipes, footwear, films, sheets and other plastic products.

Our portfolio brings together suspension and paste resins, copolymers, plasticizers, processing aids, stabilizers and specialty products. We work with our customers to understand their applications and sourcing needs.
